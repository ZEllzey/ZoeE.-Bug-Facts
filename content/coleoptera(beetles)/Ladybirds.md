## Family *Coccinellidae*

This family of [[coleoptera(beetles)/index|beetles]] is extremely important to the world of agriculture as the majority if the species feed on pest insects , like aphids, and are even utilized in biological control. There are over 5,000 different known species of Ladybird beetles, often referred to as Ladybugs in North America. The most common species are shades of yellow, orange, or red with black spots.
