## Suborder *Coleorrhyncha*

Moss [[hemiptera(true-bugs)/index|bugs]] are found only in South America, New Zealand, and Australia and there are only 25 known species. They can not fly and are often found feeding on moss or damp leaf litter in the forest floors.
