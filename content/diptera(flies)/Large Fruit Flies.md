## Family *Tephritidae*

There are over 5,000 different species of large fruit files and are considered the most biologically interesting and attractive family among [[diptera(flies)/index|true flies]] as most have intricate patterns and brightly colored wings. Their larvae are laid inside plant tissue, which they will feed on, and the adults typically do not live more than a week.
