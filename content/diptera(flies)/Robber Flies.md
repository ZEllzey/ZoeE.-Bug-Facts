## Family *Asilidae* 

The family of *Asilidae* can be characterized by the way they kill their prey. Robber [[diptera(flies)/index|flies]] will catch their prey mid flight by stabbing an injecting them with a paralytic saliva that also allows the prey to be digested. This family contains an estimated 4,000 to 7,000 species. Most in this class are described as moderately large and densely hairy.
